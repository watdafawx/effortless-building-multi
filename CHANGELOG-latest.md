## 4.2.41

What's new:
- Framed Blocks support in the Shape Generator: each part can have a camo block, applied to the framed blocks built for that part. In survival each camo costs one of that block.
- New "Smooth top: Framed slopes" option: one-step edges on the top of a shape become camouflaged framed slopes (framed slabs at corners).
- Undo and redo restore framed blocks with their camo.
- Preview shows full-cube framed blocks as their camo.

Fixes:
- Scaling a design no longer drops each part's block and repeat count.
