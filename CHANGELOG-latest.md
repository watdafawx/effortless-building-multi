## 4.2.40

What's new:
- Block Palette screen can show the selected shape as a 3D preview.
- Shape Generator: new Dome, Stairs and Pipe shapes.
- Shape Generator: towers get a door, windows, interior floors and a cone roof.
- Shape Generator: arches can be pointed (gothic); star, heart and triangle floor outlines; chevron, basket weave, wave, spiral and scatter floor patterns.
- Shape Generator: 13 ready-made Designs in the sidebar (lighthouse, watchtower, fountain, switchback stairs and more), plus tooltips on most parameters.

Fixes:
- A locked shape preview now stays locked until built or unlocked, even if building is disabled and enabled again.
